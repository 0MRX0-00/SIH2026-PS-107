1. INTRODUCTION TO COMPULSORY REGISTRATION SCHEME (CRS)
The Compulsory Registration Scheme (Scheme-II) is operated by BIS under Chapter IVA of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018. Under this scheme, manufacturers of specified electronic and IT products are required to apply for registration after getting their product tested in BIS-recognized laboratories.

2. ELIGIBILITY AND APPLICATION PROCEDURE
Clause 2.1: Who can apply?
Only actual manufacturers of the product can apply for registration. Separate registration is required for each manufacturing location/factory.

Clause 2.2: Mandatory Testing in Recognized Labs
The applicant must get the product sample tested in a laboratory recognized by BIS in India before submitting the online application on the CRS Portal (www.crsbis.in). Test reports submitted must not be older than 90 days from the date of submission of the online application.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST
Clause 3.1: Document Submissions
The applicant must submit:
a) Valid Factory Business License / Certificate of Incorporation
b) Brand / Trademark Registration Certificate or Authorization Letter
c) Test Report issued by BIS-recognized testing laboratory
d) Undertaking for compliance to technical parameters
e) Appointment of Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) for foreign manufacturers

Clause 3.2: Grant of Registration Number
Upon satisfactory scrutiny of the test reports and submitted documents, BIS issues a unique Registration Number (e.g., R-XXXXXXXX). The manufacturer is then authorized to mark the product with the Standard Registration Mark (CRS logo).
