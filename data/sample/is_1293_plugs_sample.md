# IS 1293:2019
## Indian Standard: Plugs and Socket-Outlets for Domestic and Similar General Use — Specification (Fourth Revision)

### Section 1: Scope
#### Clause 1.1 Scope Definition
This standard applies to plugs and fixed or portable socket-outlets for a.c. only, with or without earthing contact, with a rated voltage up to and including 250 V and a rated current up to and including 16 A, intended for household and similar domestic or general commercial purposes.

#### Clause 1.2 Exclusions
This standard does not cover industrial plugs and sockets conforming to IS/IEC 60309, nor appliance couplers conforming to IS/IEC 60320.

### Section 6: Standard Ratings
#### Clause 6.1 Rated Voltage and Current
Standard rated voltages are 250 V a.c. Standard rated currents are 6 A, 16 A, and 25 A. Combinations shall be as specified in Table 1.

#### Clause 6.2 Table 1 — Preferred Combinations
- 6 A, 250 V 2-pin with earth (Type D)
- 16 A, 250 V 3-pin with earth (Type M)
- 25 A, 250 V 3-pin heavy duty rating for dedicated air conditioning and heating circuits

### Section 8: Construction and Material Requirements
#### Clause 8.1 Insulating Material
Current-carrying parts and earth pins shall be enclosed in moulding of durable insulating material having high resistance to tracking, electric arc, and mechanical impact.

#### Clause 8.2 Terminal Markings and Clearances
Terminals for live (L), neutral (N), and protective earth (E) conductors shall be indelibly and clearly marked. Creepage distances between live metal parts and accessible external surfaces shall be not less than 6.0 mm.

#### Clause 8.3 Shutter Mechanism
Socket-outlets shall be provided with shutters operated automatically by the insertion of the earth pin to prevent inadvertent contact of children or metallic objects with energized contacts.
