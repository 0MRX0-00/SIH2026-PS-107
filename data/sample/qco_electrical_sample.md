# MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY
## (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade)
### ORDER: Electrical Accessories (Quality Control) Order, 2020

#### Clause 1: Short Title and Commencement
1. This order may be called the Electrical Accessories (Quality Control) Order, 2020.
2. It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.

#### Clause 2: Compulsory Use of Standard Mark
Goods or articles specified in Column (1) of the Table below shall conform to the corresponding Indian Standard specified in Column (2) and shall bear the Standard Mark (ISI Mark) under a licence from the Bureau of Indian Standards as per Scheme-I of Schedule-II of the Bureau of Indian Standards (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018.

[PAGE 2]
#### Clause 3: Table of Mandatory Goods and Applicable Standards
- Entry 1: Plugs and socket-outlets for voltages up to 250 V — Indian Standard: IS 1293 : 2019 (Title: Plugs and socket-outlets)
- Entry 2: Switches for domestic and similar purposes — Indian Standard: IS 3854 : 1997
- Entry 3: Low-voltage switchgear and controlgear — Indian Standard: IS/IEC 60947-2 : 2016

#### Clause 4: Conformity Assessment and Enforcement
The Bureau of Indian Standards shall be the certifying and enforcing authority for the goods or articles specified in the Table. Any person who contravenes the provisions of this order shall be punishable under the provisions of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.
