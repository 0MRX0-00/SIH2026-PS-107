# Indian Standard IS 6911:2017
## STAINLESS STEEL PLATE, SHEET AND STRIP — SPECIFICATION

### 1. Scope
1.1 This standard applies to hot-rolled and cold-rolled stainless steel plate, sheet, and strip used for fabrication of utensils, food contact containers, water bottles, and architectural equipment.

### 7. Chemical Composition & Steel Grades for Food Contact
7.1 Austenitic Stainless Steel Grade 304 (Designated as X6CrNi18-10, with 17.5–19.5% Chromium and 8.0–10.5% Nickel) is the authoritative food-grade stainless steel mandated for manufacturing water bottles, flasks, and kitchenware.
7.2 Grade 316 (X2CrNiMo17-12-2) with added Molybdenum (2.0–2.5%) is specified for higher salinity and acidic beverage storage.
7.3 200-series grades with low nickel and high manganese are strictly regulated and prohibited for potable acidic food contact unless explicitly tested and certified under BIS food contact regulations.

### 9. BIS Mandatory Compliance
9.1 Under the Stainless Steel Products (Quality Control) Order, all raw stainless steel materials supplied for cookware, utensils, and bottles must originate from BIS-certified steel mills with valid ISI mark licenses.
