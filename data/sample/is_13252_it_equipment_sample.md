# IS 13252 (Part 1):2010
## Indian Standard: Information Technology Equipment — Safety — Part 1: General Requirements (Second Revision)

### Section 1: Scope
#### Clause 1.1 Scope & Applicability
This standard is applicable to mains-powered or battery-powered information technology equipment, including electrical business equipment and associated equipment, with a rated voltage not exceeding 600 V. Products covered include laptops, notebook computers, tablet computers, servers, power adapters, and POS terminals.

#### Clause 1.2 Mandatory CRS Registration
Under the Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) Scheme-II mandated by MeitY, manufacturers of IT equipment must obtain BIS registration before placing products on the Indian market.

### Section 2: General Safety & Insulation
#### Clause 2.1 Electric Shock Protection
Equipment shall be designed to prevent contact with hazardous live parts under normal operation and single-fault conditions. Double or reinforced insulation must satisfy dielectric strength requirements exceeding 3000 V r.m.s.

#### Clause 2.2 Marking & Labeling
All certified equipment shall bear the BIS CRS Standard Mark along with the assigned R-number (Registration Number) and the official portal statement "Self Declaration - Conforming to IS 13252 (Part 1)".
