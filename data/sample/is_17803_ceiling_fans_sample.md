# IS 17803:2022
## Indian Standard: Electric Ceiling Type Fans and Regulators — Specification

### Section 1: Scope
#### Clause 1.1 Scope Definition
This standard specifies the performance, constructional, and safety requirements for electric ceiling type fans and their associated speed regulators intended for operation on single-phase a.c. circuits up to and including 250 V.

#### Clause 1.2 Mandatory Star Rating & Energy Efficiency
Ceiling fans covered under mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO) must comply with Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) star labeling norms in conjunction with BIS ISI Mark certification under Scheme-I.

### Section 4: Performance & Air Delivery
#### Clause 4.1 Minimum Air Delivery
The minimum air delivery for 1200 mm sweep ceiling fans shall be not less than 210 m³/min with a minimum service value of 4.0 m³/min/W for standard star-rated models.

#### Clause 4.2 Blade Safety Wire Requirement
All ceiling fans shall be equipped with an auxiliary safety wire or mechanical retention device to prevent fan blade or motor assembly detachment during operation.
