# Indian Standard IS 17526:2021
## STAINLESS STEEL VACUUM FLASKS AND INSULATED CONTAINERS — SPECIFICATION

### 1. Scope
1.1 This standard specifies the design, material, constructional, safety, and performance requirements for domestic and commercial stainless steel vacuum flasks, insulated water bottles, and beverage containers.
1.2 It applies to single-walled and double-walled stainless steel bottles used for storing and drinking water, beverages, and liquid food items.

### 4. Materials and Chemical Composition
4.1 All stainless steel parts in direct contact with potable water or foodstuffs shall be manufactured from austenitic stainless steel conforming to IS 6911, specifically Grade 304 (UNS S30400 / AISI 304 / Grade X6CrNi18-10) or Grade 316.
4.2 Plastic stoppers, lids, seals, and silicone gaskets coming into contact with food shall comply with IS 9845 (migration limits for plastics in contact with foodstuffs) and BPA-free food grade standards.
4.3 Toxic heavy metal migration (lead, cadmium, arsenic) into food simulant shall not exceed BIS permissible limits.

### 5. Mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO) & Certification
5.1 In accordance with the *Insulated Flasks, Bottles and Containers (Quality Control) Order* issued by DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, stainless steel water bottles and vacuum flasks are under **MANDATORY BIS CERTIFICATION (Scheme-I ISI Mark)**.
5.2 No manufacturer, importer, distributor, or e-commerce seller shall manufacture, import, store, or sell stainless steel water bottles in India without bearing the authentic Standard Mark (ISI mark) and the manufacturer's valid CM/L license number.

### 6. Mechanical and Performance Tests
6.1 **Thermal Insulation Test:** For double-walled vacuum flasks, boiling water filled at 95°C shall maintain at least 60°C after 6 hours and 45°C after 12 hours.
6.2 **Leakage Test:** Inverted test under 0.5 bar pressure with zero liquid droplet seepage over 15 minutes.
6.3 **Drop / Impact Test:** Bottle filled with water dropped from 1.2 meters onto a hard concrete surface shall suffer no seam fracture or leakage.
6.4 **Handle / Cap Durability:** Lids and carrying straps tested for 5,000 opening/closing cycles and 20 kg pull load.
