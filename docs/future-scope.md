# e-BIS Sahayak — Future Scope & Production Roadmap
**Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement SIH26107**  
*AI-Powered Intelligent Assistant for Indian Standards & BIS Services*

---

## 1. Roadmap & Expansion Opportunities

### Phase 1: National Scale Knowledge Ingestion
- Ingestion of all **21,000+ Indian Standards** across all 15 Technical Divisions of BIS (Civil, Chemical, Electrotechnical, Food & Agriculture, Mechanical, Medical, Petroleum, Textiles, etc.).
- Automated scrapers & webhooks synchronizing with DPIIT and BIS e-Gazette publications for real-time QCO effective date updates.

### Phase 2: All 22 Scheduled Indian Languages via Bhashini AI
- Full integration with the Government of India's **Bhashini AI Platform** (ASR, NMT, and TTS models) to enable voice-to-voice query and response across all 22 official Indian languages.

### Phase 3: Manakonline Portal API Integration & Auto-Filing
- Direct single-sign-on (SSO) integration with BIS Manakonline portal (`manakonline.in`).
- AI-assisted pre-application document readiness checks (analyzing test reports against standard clauses before submission).

### Phase 4: Vision & Multimodal Inspection
- Visual inspection of ISI mark packaging and Hallmarking stamps using multi-modal models to identify counterfeit markings or incorrect standard labeling.

### Phase 5: Voicebot & WhatsApp / SMS Sahayak
- Deployment of a verified WhatsApp Business API bot and IVR voice hotline enabling rural artisans, MSME owners, and consumer citizens to access BIS intelligence via phone calls or messaging.
